## 1. Overview & Policy

All audio recordings in the Tier A benchmark corpus were generated and contributed directly by the SautiCivic core research and development team (**Agoro Timilehin / drizzy765** and **David Akhuabe**). 

### Consent Principles
- **Informed & Voluntary:** Speakers participated voluntarily with full knowledge of how the audio data is processed, evaluated, and published.
- **Academic & Open Research Use:** Data is collected solely for benchmarking automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems (Sahara v2.5, Whisper large-v3, Deepgram Nova-3) on Nigerian Pidgin / English code-switched civic and legal grievance intake.
- **Zero Citizen PII:** All recordings follow synthetic prompt scenarios. No real citizen names, private telephone numbers, residential street numbers, or sensitive identifying information are contained in the transcripts or audio.
- **Licensing & Distribution:** Audio data is licensed for non-commercial research, benchmarking, and demonstration purposes under the project's open research terms.
